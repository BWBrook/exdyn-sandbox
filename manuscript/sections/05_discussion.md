The central outcome of this workflow is not merely a fitted model; it is a
transparent map of where inference is and is not supported.

Three practical lessons emerge:

- performance tractability must be engineered explicitly for large spatial
  domains;
- identifiability must be demonstrated under synthetic truth before ecological
  claims on real data;
- low-information real-data outcomes are scientifically meaningful when failure
  modes are diagnosed and documented.

Future extensions can add habitat covariates and alternative analysis-unit
representations, but only after preserving the same canonical and gate-driven
discipline.
